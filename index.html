🍛   About us   🍛

Fifteen years of the Nahasapeemapetilon family kitchen

✦ Who we are

We are the Nahasapeemapetilon family, and PAALHU is our kitchen opened up to the neighbourhood. Our father is in charge of the stoves, where he has spent more than 30 years cooking, and our mother takes care of the dining room and welcomes every guest at the door. The two of us grew up between these tables, and today we are the ones who greet you when you walk in. Altogether we are a small team of nine people who treat the restaurant as an extension of our own home.

✦ How PAALHU started

The idea was born in [city / region of India], where our parents grew up watching our grandmother grind her own spices every morning. When the family arrived in Madrid, they realised how much they missed those flavours, so they started cooking them for friends at home. There were more and more guests each time, and 15 years ago, in 2011, they decided to take the leap and open our own restaurant at Calle de Pirineos 55.

We named it PAALHU because [explain here what the name means to you: a family name, a place, a word in your language]. Since that first day in 2011 we have kept the same promise: recipes made the way they are made at home, spices we blend ourselves, and nothing frozen.

🔥   Spice guide   🔥

Every dish on the menu can be cooked to the heat you like. This is the scale we use, from the gentlest to the fiercest — just tell us the number when you order.

Our five levels of heat
Level Chillies Name What to expect
1 🌶 Very mild Creamy and sweet. Perfect for children.
2 🌶🌶 Mild Full of spice aroma, almost no burn.
3 🌶🌶🌶 Medium A warm, steady heat. Our most ordered level.
4 🌶🌶🌶🌶 🔥 Hot Real chilli heat. Bring a mango lassi.
5 🌶🌶🌶🌶🌶 🔥🔥 Very hot For chilli lovers only. You have been warned.
